Nightcarlo AI Opportunity Radar

Discovering practical AI business opportunities and marketing solutions for small businesses.

About the Project

Nightcarlo AI Opportunity Radar is an independent research and content planning project currently in early development and testing.

Research Focus

AI entrepreneurship, real business experiences, open-source AI tools, and small-business marketing experiments.

Current Capabilities

The prototype supports GitHub discovery, manually imported research sources, relevance screening and editable video planning templates.

Automated Reddit API access is not currently enabled and would require appropriate authorization.

Planned Content and Data Use

The project may support original Chinese-language educational videos, AI tool demonstrations, and future commercial services.

It is not intended for resale of raw Reddit data. Research claims require verification and privacy protections.
